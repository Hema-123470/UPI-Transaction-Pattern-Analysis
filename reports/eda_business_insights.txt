==============================================================
  UPI TRANSACTION PATTERN ANALYSIS
  Phase 2 – Key Business Insights
==============================================================

INSIGHT 1 – Scale:
  The dataset covers 10,000 UPI transactions worth ₹149,824,746 (≈ ₹14.98 Crore) over Jan–Sep 2024.

INSIGHT 2 – Reliability:
  87.6% of transactions succeeded. Failure rate is 9.3%, which translates to 929 failed transactions and a potential revenue leakage of ₹13,986,974.

INSIGHT 3 – Peak Usage:
  Transactions peak at 04:00 hrs (457 transactions). 'Night' is the busiest time-of-day segment. Banks and support teams should staff up during these hours.

INSIGHT 4 – Merchant Categories:
  'Salary/Transfer' generates the highest transaction value.
  'Rent' has the most transactions by count.
  'Emi/Loan' has the highest failure rate (10.6%) — investigate payment gateway issues.

INSIGHT 5 – Payment Type:
  P2M (merchant payments) account for 55.7% of count but 46.7% of total value (₹69,979,404), indicating higher ticket sizes in merchant payments.

INSIGHT 6 – Geography & Banking:
  'Tamil Nadu' leads in transaction count. 'Hdfc Bank' is the top sender bank. 'Paytm' is the most-used UPI app.

INSIGHT 7 – Weekend Spending:
  Weekend average transaction amount (₹15,167) is higher than weekdays (₹14,911) by 1.7%, suggesting leisure/entertainment spending peaks on weekends.

INSIGHT 8 – High-Value Transactions:
  4,507 transactions (45.1%) are high-value (>₹10,000), accounting for ₹89.0% of total value. Top categories: Salary/Transfer, Rent, Emi/Loan.

INSIGHT 9 – Monthly Growth:
  Transaction value grew from ₹17,769,773 (January) to ₹16,673,114 (September), a decline of 6.2%.

INSIGHT 10 – Device Adoption:
  'Android' dominates with 60.4% of transactions. Feature Phone (*99#) usage reflects UPI adoption in semi-urban/rural areas.
